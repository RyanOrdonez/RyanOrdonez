### About
I run operations at [House of Maevie](https://houseofmaevie.com/), a full-service interior design studio in Raleigh, NC. The studio does major renovations, additions, and new custom homes across the Triangle, working alongside builders and architects from concept through construction and furnishing.

My job is the business behind the design: how projects move from inquiry to install, how work is scheduled and tracked, how vendors and trades are coordinated, and how the numbers get reported. I'm now building that out as **House of Maevie Business Systems Design**, the operational systems, automation, and data tools that let a creative studio grow without losing its craft.

### What I'm Building: House of Maevie Business Systems
- **Client pipeline:** a structured flow from inquiry through consultation, proposal, contract, and kickoff, so every client gets the same high-touch experience
- **Project operations:** standard workflows for design phases, procurement, and install, with clear handoffs between designers, builders, architects, and vendors
- **Procurement & vendor tracking:** orders, lead times, deliveries, and budgets in one place instead of scattered spreadsheets and email threads
- **Reporting & dashboards:** project status, capacity, and financial metrics that give leadership a clear view of the business
- **Automation:** repetitive admin (scheduling, follow-ups, document generation) automated so the team spends its time on design
